# 0038. A creative as a workspace: Settings and Stats, an inspector, a template sheet

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-06
- Amends: [0037](0037-reduced-navigation-and-copy.md) — delivers what it listed as planned (the template picker, the workspace, search on Creatives); [0011](0011-conditional-grouped-config-schemas.md), [0030](0030-duel-template.md), [0031](0031-step-builder-and-forwarded-picks.md) — how their groups are drawn; [0008](0008-catalog-first-information-architecture.md) — where `/dashboard` sends an account that has no creatives; [0013](0013-public-free-tools-section.md) and [0035](0035-vast-video-creatives.md) — the tools' bar and how the generator's form is laid out

## Context

The first step of the redesign (ADR-0037) cut the navigation, the landing and the text. Three of the owner's complaints were left, and they were measured on the build of 2026-11-06 at 1280×720:

- **Settings sprawl.** A creative's settings were one long form: 1.1 screens for Scratch & Reveal, 2.2 for Duel, 4.6 for Step Builder (5.7 with the preview beside it). Sections stacked, the outcome matrix and the options tables inside them, and the live player sat in a sticky column that scrolled with the page.
- **Statistics buried.** A creative's page was 1.5 screens; the VAST tag was its fourth block, conversions and errors lay below the fold, and *Copy tag* — the action most people open a creative for — needed a scroll.
- **Three pages to start.** Creating a creative was catalog → template page → *Configure creative*: three clicks and three page loads before the first field.

## Decision

**A creative is a workspace with two sections, Settings and Stats.** Both are full-viewport screens with a bar of their own (`WorkspaceBar`, 48px) in place of the product bar: the way back to *Creatives*, the name, the format and the serving state, the **Settings | Stats** switch, and on the right *More*, *Copy tag* and the screen's one primary. The two sections keep their addresses (`/dashboard/creatives/[id]/edit`, `/dashboard/creatives/[id]`), so every existing link lands where it did. The product bar stays on the list-like pages (Creatives, the postback, the accounts) through `DashboardShell`; the dashboard layout keeps only the check that there is a signed-in viewer.

**Settings is an outline, a stage and an inspector.** The outline lists a template's sections — a schema group is a section, except that an `options` or a `matrix` group belongs to the group before it — with a rail that says whether a section is complete. The stage is the player well as large as the screen leaves it, with the player chosen from a menu. The inspector shows one section's fields in 340px. At 1280×720 a section is one screen. Templates of six to eight fields have no outline: one inspector. What the form posts is unchanged — every active field under its own name, the same Server Actions, the same `buildConfigFromValues` — and the sections the inspector is not showing post through hidden inputs. A save the browser would have refused — a required field empty, a URL that is not an address, a number outside its bounds — in one of them is caught first, and the section, the field and the reason are shown.

**The stage is outside the form.** The Sandbox player runs the creative in the page's own document, inside the stage, and a creative's buttons carry no `type` — to any form around them each is a submit button. With the stage inside the form, a click on the ad's call to action would have saved the creative (and, in edit mode, published the unsaved edits to the live tag). The `<form>` wraps the inspector's fields and nothing else; the bar's name, format and save button join it by its id.

**Stats is one screen.** Delivery, viewability and errors; the conversions strip; errors by code and conversions by exit in two panels, three rows each and *Show all* in place; by-goal and by-day behind disclosures. *Copy tag* is always in the bar, and is the screen's accent.

**New creative is a sheet.** *New creative* opens a dialog over the list — the templates and, beside them, the selected one's preview, standards and description — and *Configure creative* goes to the workspace on its first section. The VAST video generator is the last row. `/dashboard/creatives/new` without a template opens the list with the sheet open, and the catalog's own links (`/catalog`, `/catalog/[slug]`) are unchanged.

**An account lives on Creatives.** `/dashboard` — still the default destination after sign-in and sign-up — redirects to Creatives for everyone. ADR-0008 sent an account with no creatives to the catalog, because an empty table was a bad first screen; the empty list is now a designed one (the title and one button that opens the sheet) and is where the access notice is shown, and the catalog is no longer in the signed-in navigation.

**Creatives gets search and a filter by state, and per-row *Copy tag*, *Edit*, *More*.** Client-side over the rows the page already has: an account's creatives are dozens. *More* (the validator, duplicate, delete) is the same menu as the workspace's, portalled in a row, whose scroll frame would clip it.

**The tools get a bar of their own, and the generator is a workspace too.** `ToolBar` — the lockup, the **Validator | Generator** switch, the visitor — replaces the product bar on `/tools/vast-validator` and `/tools/vast-generator`, which stay public. Below `md` it re-stacks, as the workspace bar does — the lockup and the account on one row, the switch with *Creatives* or the language control on the next — because the switch is about 184px and a single row at 360 ran it under the account. The VAST generator's form, which ran to 2.8 screens, becomes the same outline, stage and inspector: the tag's sections one at a time, the player with a *Player | XML* switch on the stage, *Create creative* at the foot of the inspector, and a save the browser can already refuse opens the section with the error. Editing a video creative at `/dashboard/creatives/[id]/edit` is the same screen under a creative's own bar, with Settings | Stats. The validator keeps its instrument layout under the new bar.

**The postback is one panel.** The URL with *Copy URL* and *Rotate key*; the parameter and status tables behind a disclosure; the log. The three-paragraph how-to is gone — what it said is in the parameter table's click-id row and in the click-through field's macros.

## Consequences

- `ConfiguratorForm`, `ScenarioGrid`, `OutcomeMatrix` and `CreativesTable` are replaced by `ConfiguratorWorkspace`, the inspector's `OptionRows` / `ScenarioRows` / `OutcomeDrill` and `CreativesList`. A template needs no code: the sections come from its `config_schema` groups, and no schema changed — there is no migration and nothing to backfill.
- The workspace is an exception to "every page goes through `ui/Container`" and to a persistent product bar. The design system says so (§5, §6 "Workspace"): *Creatives* in the bar is the navigation there, at every width.
- A creative's settings can no longer be skimmed in one scroll; they are read a section at a time. The outline's rail is what keeps the whole in view — a section turns `live` when its required fields are filled, and says so with a check and in words as well as in colour.
- A save is refused in the browser for a missing required field, a URL that is not an address or a number out of bounds wherever it is, with its place named (`A → B · Heading`), where it used to be refused by the server with the whole form lost — or, for the URL, not refused at all: the server rejects only a script scheme, and a hidden input is exempt from the browser's `type="url"`. The server's check stays, as the only one that counts.
- *Show all*, the disclosures and the sheet are client state over server-rendered rows; no new data is read. The Stats page reads what it read.
- The validator is not rebuilt as three panes (input and verdict, report in tabs, player and events): it fits one screen as it is, and the report's timeline carries URLs that a 340px pane cannot. It is the one item of ADR-0037's plan left open, and a candidate for the next amendment.
- Phones: the workspace bar re-stacks, the stage sticks to the top — only where the screen is 600px high or more, and with its screen capped by the screen's height, since a phone on its side is 360px high — the primary moves to a strip at the foot, the outline is a row of chips, and the Creatives list is rows with a bottom *New creative*. The generator's phone layout is its own (the stage first, the chips, only the buttons at the foot of the screen) and the tools' bar re-stacks; the validator's page is what it already was.
- The creative name in the bar is a dense mono input (32px, `inputDenseClass`), not a sans line with a dashed underline: §4 makes every text input mono and §6 sets a control's height, and a 1.45:1 underline was not an affordance.
- A creative's own buttons are not the app's to type. The runtime templates build `<button>`s without `type`; the slider guards its own, the others do not. Keeping the stage out of the form is the fix here; adding `type="button"` to the templates is a runtime change (`runtime:push`) and a hardening of its own.
- Not done: a guard against leaving Settings with unsaved edits (the *Stats* tab and *Creatives* are plain links, as the old form's way out was), and the validator as three panes.
